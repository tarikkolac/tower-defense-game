🏰 Tower Defense

Goblin Invasion

🎯 About the Game

Defend your castle against waves of goblin invaders! Build and upgrade towers strategically to stop the enemy horde. This pixel-art tower defense game features 10 different goblin types, 3 unique tower types with upgrades, and increasingly challenging waves.

📢 Note: This is a GDevelop project. To play the game, open the game.json file in GDevelop and click the Play button, or export it for web using GDevelop's export feature.
Download GDevelop

👾 10 Enemy Types

From quick scouts to tanky brutes, each goblin has unique abilities and stats.

🏗️ 3 Tower Types

Archer, Mortar, and Barracks - each with 3 upgrade levels.

🌊 10 Waves

Progressive difficulty with more and stronger enemies each wave.

💰 Economy System

Earn gold by defeating enemies, spend it wisely on towers and upgrades.

🎮 How to Play

  • Click on a tower button (Archer, Mortar, or Barracks) to select it
  • Click on a green tower spot to build the selected tower
  • Press the Play button to start a wave
  • Defend your castle! Each enemy that reaches it costs 1 life
  • Survive all 10 waves to win!
